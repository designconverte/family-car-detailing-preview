Process

Three layers.One standard.

Three blades, one F: wash it clean, correct what daily life left behind, protect the result. Outside, inside and under the hood.

01 / 03Hand wash · outside

Wash

Foam, a wash mitt and two hands, panel by panel: body, wheels, tires and wheel wells. Then a hand dry, so nothing is left to spot.

02 / 03Interior deep clean

Correct

Inside, we go after what daily life leaves behind. Seats and carpets get deep-cleaned; the wheel, dash, console and doors get brushed by hand.

03 / 03Under the hood · final check

Protect

With the engine bay add-on, rubber and plastics get a heat-resistant protective sealant. Then a last pass inside, so the car goes home ready for the family.

How much does car detailing cost in Waltham?
A full detail is $200 for sedans, $249 for SUVs and starts at $349 for trucks. Engine bay cleaning is $149 and headlight restoration is $139. Paint polishing is quoted after we see the car. We confirm the price with you before we start.

Do I need an appointment for car detailing in Waltham?
Yes, booking ahead holds a slot in the studio for your car. You can request a day online or call (781) 290-8518. We're open [HOURS].
What is included in a full car detail?
A full detail covers the car inside and out. Inside: a complete vacuum, carpets and mats, seats, dashboard and console, doors and interior trim. Outside: a full body wash, wheels and tires, wheel wells, then drying and a final finish.
How long does the engine bay protection last?
Up to about three months. It depends on how the car is used and maintained and on the product's specifications. The protection is heat-resistant and does not come off easily in normal use.
Does paint polishing remove all scratches?
No. Polishing reduces or removes light scratches and brings back gloss, but a scratch that goes through the clear coat can't be polished out. We look at the paint with you and agree on the price and the expected result before we start.
Can you get pet hair and kid stains out of a car interior?
In most cases, yes; pet hair, crumbs, spills, and ground-in dirt are what the interior clean is for. Stains or odors that have set for a long time may fade rather than disappear. If we see one like that, we tell you up front.
Is there car detailing near me in Waltham, MA?
Yes, our studio is at 42 Felton Street in Waltham. It's a short drive from Newton, Lexington, Watertown, Belmont, Weston, and Lincoln. You bring the car to us, and the work is done in the studio.
